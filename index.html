Hello Hunger

A home-cooked meal platform built from scratch for the Indian market. Three apps, one business, designed and built end to end.

Consumer appHome chef appAdmin app

The problem

Talented home cooks had no easy way to reach hungry neighbours or run a real food business.

People who wanted fresh, home-cooked meals had nowhere simple to find them, and no way to know who was in the kitchen.

The solution

Customers browse home chefs nearby and order meals in the consumer app.

Chefs run their business from their own app: orders, pickup and preparation.

An admin app handles accounts, approvals, background checks and a command center for the whole platform.

Bridging the gap

Before building anything, I sat down with people on both sides: home chefs and the families who wanted to eat their food.

I listened for what worried them and what they would need to trust a stranger's kitchen or a stranger's order. Trust came first, so approvals and background checks sit at the center of the product.

ShipPick

A platform that stops packages from getting stolen. A simple idea with a real impact.

Mobile appReal-time OTP

The problem

Packages left at the door get stolen, and waiting at home for every delivery is not an option for most people.

The idea

Ship your package to the nearest onboarded store instead of your doorstep.

How it works

The store receives it and holds it safely.

You pick it up with a one-time code generated in real time, so only you can collect it.

ServiceCircle

A trusted network of service providers, built from the people you already trust.

ReferralsShared contacts

The problem

People constantly ask friends and family for a good plumber, tutor or electrician. The answers live in scattered chats and memory.

The idea

Share the contacts of service providers you have worked with, with the friends and family who need them.

How it works

Save the providers you have used, share them with your circle, and find the next one through someone you trust.
